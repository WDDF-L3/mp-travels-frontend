RECRUITMENT PROCESS

A streamlined selection procedure ensuring the right talent is matched with the right opportunity.

Pre-Labor Approval

MP Travels Ltd. Upon receiving the authorized Demand Letter from the respective company, MP Travels Limited submits the documents to the Bureau of Manpower Employment and Training (BMET) for pre-labor approval. BMET reviews and approves the documents for further processing.

Advertisement

Once pre-approved by BMET, the Demand Letter is advertised in national newspapers. MP Travels Limited also uses digital platforms, SMS, and calls to reach candidates and collect documents. Applications can be submitted directly or via Public Relations Officers.

Candidate Screening

We review our up-to-date database to identify potential candidates based on their qualifications, skills, and experience according to employer requirements. Final interviews are conducted by the employer or their representative and may include oral, written, and practical tests.

Communication

All departments at MP Travels Limited are fully computerized and networked to ensure fast and reliable service for clients and candidates. Our dedicated staff is always available to provide quality manpower support.

Medical Checkup

Shortlisted candidates are sent for a full medical examination at government-authorized medical centers. Only those deemed physically and mentally fit are eligible to sign the employment contract and proceed to the next step.

Visa Processing

We compile and send all required documents for visa processing—such as passport copies, photographs, medical reports, and experience certificates—to the employer.

Orientation

Once candidates receive the job offer or employment visa, we conduct an orientation session. This helps create awareness and build a strong relationship between employer and employee. Candidates are informed about the company, workplace environment, job responsibilities, and expectations before departure.

Final Labor Approval

All necessary documents—such as the original passport, visa copy (or paper visa), medical report, orientation certificate, and insurance policy—are submitted to BMET for final labor approval and immigration clearance. BMET reviews and approves the documents accordingly.

Travel Arrangements

All departments at MP Travels Limited are fully computerized and networked to ensure fast and reliable service for clients and candidates. Our dedicated staff is always available to provide quality manpower support.
